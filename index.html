SANTISIMA FU3GA

DJ, Producer & Multimedia Artist

> 01_BIOGRAPHY

Santisima Fu3ga is a Uruguay-born DJ, producer, and cultural organizer based in the west of France, bridging mainstream Latin frequencies with cutting-edge alternative producers. Moving fluidly through latin club, neo perreo, baile funk, and latin core, her sets deliver high-energy, versatile dancefloor experiences that defy single-note atmospheres and keep rooms constantly shifting.

As the founder and curator of the Santa Muerte Perreo collective, her practice is deeply anchored in community advocacy for the Latin American migrant and queer diaspora. Transforming the dancefloor into a shared, high-voltage ritual, she creates vital spaces of resistance, visibility, and alternative club culture. Simultaneously expanding into sound production and multimedia installations, Santisima Fu3ga translates raw physical club urgency into sharp, austere artistic statements.
